Skeleton Park Crafters

A queer & inclusive crafting community in Kingston, Ontario

About Us

We started out in 2022 as the Skeleton Park Knitters, meeting weekly at Skeleton Park in Kingston. In 2026, we rebranded as the Skeleton Park Crafters to be more explicitly diverse and inclusive. We also decided on a rotating schedule of locations so that we would have more options, particularly in inclement weather. In addition to our weekly Sunday meetups, we occasionally organize workshops and trips.

For our community forum and up to date information on location changes, please join us on Discord and Instagram.

Location Schedule

We meet every week on Sundays from 11-1.

In general, we will be in the following set locations every month. If there are any disruptions (i.e. holidays, weather interruptions, the business has another event that day) we will do our best to post updates here, on our Discord server and on Instagram

  • 1st Sunday of the month: Sens Cafe in the meeting room downstairs.
  • 2nd Sunday of the month: Skeleton Park weather permitting or 12 Cat Studios. Use the first door on the opposite side of the building to the climbing gym, the studio is on the second floor. You can park in the alley on the side of the building.
  • 3rd Sunday of the month: Something in the Water Brewing Co./Ghost Taco
  • 4th Sunday of the month: BSE Skateboard Shop + Coffee Bar
  • 5th Sunday of the month: Check on Discord for updates.

Code of Conduct

  1. Bring a craft - Please bring a project to work on. You don’t have to knit - any art/craft is welcome, such as crochet, sketching, embroidery, beadwork, etc.
  2. Be inclusive - All crafts, all ages, all skill levels, all people are welcome. Discrimination, hate speech, bullying, and unkindness are not tolerated. We welcome new members openly.
  3. No promotions or spam - Sharing a new on-topic project or venture in the Knitting Chatter thread is fine, but this is not the space for advertising, self-promotion, or topics not related to SPC or crafts.
  4. Be respectful - Be aware of how your topics of conversation might affect others, and consider if SPC is the right venue. We are a pro-science and pro-vaccine group. This is not a space for conspiracy theories or medical/political misinformation.
  5. Care for our space - whether indoors at a studio, business, or at the park, be aware of and respectful to others using the space. Leave the space better than you found it.
  6. Ask permission before sharing photos - if you have great pics you want to share on social media, please ask before posting if your photo shows anyone’s faces.
